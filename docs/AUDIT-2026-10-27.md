## Overall assessment

The requested public website exists: six primary pages, lion initiative detail, legal pages, responsive navigation, local typography, GSAP motion, metadata, sitemap, robots and a validated enquiry flow. It is a usable foundation, with outstanding launch configuration and verification. CMS, accounts, database and payments are deliberately outside version-one scope.

## Outstanding items, in priority order

1. **High — CI has no successful verification.** The initial run 40484949524 failed; its verify job has zero executed steps and no assigned runner. Log retrieval returned 404. The cause is unconfirmed; review GitHub's run/check annotations and account Actions settings. Local checks do not resolve the hosted CI failure.
2. **High — Direct enquiry delivery needs operational setup.** `src/lib/enquiries.ts` requires SITE_URL, ENQUIRIES_ENABLED, RESEND_API_KEY, ENQUIRY_FROM and both Turnstile keys. ENQUIRY_TO is optional. Source cannot establish the deployed environment. Without configuration, the form prepares a mailto draft; it does not deliver a website submission. Verify sender domain, permitted Turnstile hostname, staff inbox and actual delivery before activation.
3. **High — Privacy approval remains incomplete.** `/privacy` explicitly says no fixed inbox retention period has been published. Confirm the period, staff access and final notice before enabling direct collection.
4. **Medium — Production domain and crawl settings need verification.** `src/lib/seo.ts` falls back to localhost; layout disables indexing without SITE_URL. Set the final HTTPS domain before build, then verify canonical URLs, sitemap, robots and social previews on the intended host.
5. **Medium — Browser regression/accessibility automation is absent.** Tests cover validation and the enquiry route, not keyboard navigation, mobile focus trapping, route transitions, responsive image crops, contrast or screen-reader use. Add Playwright and an accessibility scan, and verify actual phones before launch. Previous manual verification documents are historical evidence, not a rerun of this revision.
6. **Medium — Organisation-specific credibility material is incomplete.** About contains founder information, but no complete approved governance roster, founder portrait, registration evidence or genuine field-activity gallery. Publish verified details only when available; retain proposed project/registration disclosures.
7. **Low — News is a planning placeholder.** The homepage has a planning-stage update, not a dated news/archive system. Static dated updates can be added without introducing a CMS.
8. **Low — Enquiry resilience is intentionally basic.** Rate limiting is per process and not shared across serverless instances. There is no durable outbox, delivery webhook tracking or submission idempotency. Turnstile is the primary bot control; consider shared limits and delivery monitoring if volume grows.
